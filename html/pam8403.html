Discription: This project shows how to build a simple audio amplifier using the PAM8403 module and a 3.7v lithium-ion battery . The amplifier can drive two 4ohm speakers 4ohm speakers and has a good sound quality for its low cost. You can find more details about the components and the circuit below.

Things Required: Pam8403 module, Tp4056 module, 2 speakers 4Ω each, 3.5mm audio jack/aux, tools.
